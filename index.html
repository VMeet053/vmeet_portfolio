About

Hi, I'm Meet. I've been building websites professionally since 2022, mostly Shopify stores and WordPress sites, plus the occasional React app or CRM when a client needs something custom.

I spent my first two years at Go Higher Infotech, working remotely on client projects. In 2024 I started taking on clients directly, which is how I ended up building stores for brands in Denmark, Norway and Sweden as well as here in India.

These days I run Hexafusion Infotech with Disha Hingu, who looks after the technical side as our CTO. I still write code, but a good part of my week now goes into client calls, estimates and keeping projects on schedule.

What I usually work with: Shopify and Liquid, WordPress, React, Next.js, Node.js, PHP, Tailwind, and Vercel or AWS for hosting.

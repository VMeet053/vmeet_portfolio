01 — Profile

Engineering-led leadership.

Vasoya Meet is the Chief Executive Officer of Hexafusion Infotech, where he oversees client relationships, business planning and project delivery. He leads the company alongside Chief Technology Officer Disha Hingu.

A full stack developer by training, he began his career in 2022 at Go Higher Infotech and moved into independent practice in 2024, delivering e-commerce and web platforms for brands in India, Denmark, Norway and Sweden. He remains closely involved in the company's Shopify and front-end engineering.

He holds a Master of Computer Applications from Bhagwan Mahavir University.

“I care about the details that clients feel — how fast a page loads, how easy checkout is, and how simple it is for a team to update content.”
